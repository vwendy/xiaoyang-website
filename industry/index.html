// what I do

From raw signals to decisions

01

Behavioral metrics from sensor data

Turn passive phone and wearable streams into interpretable features that track mood, well-being, and risk.

02

Study design & measurement

Protocols, surveys, and event-triggered sampling that produce data you can trust, then the analysis to read it.

03

Sensing apps & dashboards

Android apps for passive tracking and in-the-moment surveys, with R Shiny dashboards on top.

04

AI for well-being

Prototype generative-AI assistants, like one that helps people manage their screen time and relationship with their phone.
